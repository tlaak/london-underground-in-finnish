# Northolt

Northolt is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Ealing in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Northolt                   | Pohjoiskulma        |

## Background

Despite the modern spelling, Northolt is not "north wood." [It is recorded in 872 as _norð healum_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northolt), "the northern nook" — _norð_ (north) plus _healh_ (a nook, corner, or sheltered spot). The final _-holt_ evolved from _healh_ and has no connection to _holt_, "wood."

## Direct translations

| English                | Finnish  |
| ---------------------- | -------- |
| north (_norð_)         | pohjois- |
| nook, corner (_healh_) | kulma    |
| wood (folk reading)    | metsä    |

The trap is to read _-holt_ as "wood" (_metsä_) and produce a "north wood." The true element is _healh_, a nook or corner — Finnish _kulma_. So the faithful translation is _Pohjoiskulma_, "north corner," not _Pohjoismetsä_.

## Conclusion

Northolt station in Finnish shall be called _Pohjoiskulma_.
