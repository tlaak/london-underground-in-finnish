# Stratford

Stratford is served by the Central and Jubilee lines, in the London Borough of Newham in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Stratford                  | Katuvieska          |

## Background

[Stratford is first recorded as _Strætforda_ in 1067 and means "ford on a Roman road"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratford,_London) — from Old English _stræt_ (a Roman road, giving modern "street") and _ford_. The road ran from Aldgate across the River Lea towards Romford and Colchester.

## Direct translations

| English               | Finnish           |
| --------------------- | ----------------- |
| _stræt_ (street/road) | katu              |
| ford                  | vieska (kahlaamo) |

_Stræt_ is _katu_ (street), and _ford_ is again _vieska_, the project's ford-element (as in [Woodford](woodford.md) → _Metsävieska_). Together they give _Katuvieska_, "street ford."

## Conclusion

Stratford station in Finnish shall be called _Katuvieska_.
