# Leyton

Leyton is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Waltham Forest in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Leyton                     | Jokela              |

## Background

[Leyton means "the settlement (_tūn_) on the River Lea"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leyton), and was known as _Low Leyton_ until 1921. The first element is the river-name Lea; the second is the common Old English _tūn_, a farm or settlement.

## Direct translations

| English            | Finnish |
| ------------------ | ------- |
| Lea (the river)    | joki    |
| _tūn_ (settlement) | -la     |

The Lea is a specific river, but for a settlement named simply as "the place by the river" the natural Finnish rendering treats it as just that: _joki_ (river) with the settlement-suffix _-la_. The result, _Jokela_, is a genuine and common Finnish place name (there is a well-known Jokela in Tuusula) meaning exactly "the place by the river."

## Conclusion

Leyton station in Finnish shall be called _Jokela_.
