# Holborn

Holborn is served by the Central and Piccadilly lines, in the London Borough of Camden (with a small part in the City of London).

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Holborn                    | Notkopuro           |

## Background

[Holborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holborn) derives from the Middle English _hol_ ("hollow") and _bourne_ ("brook"), referring to the River Fleet where it ran through a steep hollow. The name is thus "the brook in the hollow."

## Direct translations

| English          | Finnish |
| ---------------- | ------- |
| _hol_ (hollow)   | notko   |
| _bourne_ (brook) | puro    |

_Hol_ is a hollow or dell, in Finnish _notko_; _bourne_ is a brook, _puro_. Together they give _Notkopuro_, "hollow brook" — a small, plausible Finnish stream-name of exactly the kind that becomes a place name.

## Conclusion

Holborn station in Finnish shall be called _Notkopuro_.
