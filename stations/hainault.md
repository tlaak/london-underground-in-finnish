# Hainault

Hainault is served by the Central line (Hainault loop), in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Hainault                   | Pitäjänmetsä        |

## Background

Despite its French look, Hainault has nothing to do with the Belgian county of Hainaut. [The name is Old English for "wood belonging to a religious community"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hainault,_London) — the wood held by the nuns of Barking Abbey — from _higna_ ("of the community, of the household") and _holt_ ("wood"). The spelling was altered from the 17th century through a false association with Philippa of Hainault, queen of Edward III.

## Direct translations

| English                    | Finnish |
| -------------------------- | ------- |
| _higna_ (of the community) | pitäjä  |
| _holt_ (wood)              | metsä   |

_Holt_ is a wood, _metsä_. For _higna_ — the religious household or community that owned the wood — the closest Finnish handle is _pitäjä_, historically a parish or the community of a parish. _Pitäjänmetsä_, "the parish's wood," captures the sense of a wood held in common by a religious community, and reads as a natural Finnish name.

## Conclusion

Hainault station in Finnish shall be called _Pitäjänmetsä_.
