# Newbury Park

Newbury Park is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Newbury Park               | Uudenlinnanpuisto   |

## Background

Newbury Park carries the common English place-name elements _new_ and [_bury_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newbury_Park_tube_station) — the latter from Old English _burh_, a fortified town or stronghold — with "Park" added later.

## Direct translations

| English                     | Finnish |
| --------------------------- | ------- |
| new                         | uusi    |
| _bury_ (_burh_, stronghold) | linna   |
| park                        | puisto  |

As at [Kingsbury](kingsbury.md), _bury_ (_burh_) is a fortified place, _linna_, rather than a plain town. "New burh" is therefore _Uusilinna_, and with _puisto_ ("park") the whole becomes _Uudenlinnanpuisto_, "new-castle park." The pattern echoes the real Finnish town [Uusikaupunki](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uusikaupunki), "new town."

## Conclusion

Newbury Park station in Finnish shall be called _Uudenlinnanpuisto_.
