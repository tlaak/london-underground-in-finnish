# Queensway

Queensway is served by the Central line, in Bayswater in the City of Westminster.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Queensway                  | Kuningattarentie    |

## Background

The street was originally Black Lion Lane. [It was renamed Queen's Road after 1837 in honour of Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensway,_London), who is said to have ridden along it, and was later restyled Queensway. The name honours the queen directly.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish    |
| ------- | ---------- |
| queen   | kuningatar |
| way     | tie        |

Both words are plain: _queen_ is _kuningatar_ and _way_ is _tie_. In the genitive the compound is _Kuningattarentie_, "the queen's way" — the same construction as the Underground's other royal name, [Kingsbury](kingsbury.md) → _Kuninkaanlinna_.

## Conclusion

Queensway station in Finnish shall be called _Kuningattarentie_.
