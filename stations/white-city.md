# White City

White City is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Hammersmith and Fulham, in the northern part of Shepherd's Bush.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| White City                 | Valkoinenkaupunki   |

## Background

[White City is named after the "Great White City"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_City,_London) — the exhibition grounds built for the 1908 Franco-British Exhibition and Olympic Games, whose pavilions were clad in white, marble-like fibrous plaster.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish   |
| ------- | --------- |
| white   | valkoinen |
| city    | kaupunki  |

Both words are plain: _white_ is _valkoinen_ and _city_ is _kaupunki_. Unlike the fortified _-bury_ names, this really is a "city," so _kaupunki_ is right. The compound is _Valkoinenkaupunki_, "white city."

## Conclusion

White City station in Finnish shall be called _Valkoinenkaupunki_.
