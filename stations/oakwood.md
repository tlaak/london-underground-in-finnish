# Oakwood

Oakwood is served by the Piccadilly line, in the London Borough of Enfield in North London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Oakwood                    | Tammimetsä          |

## Background

Oakwood is a modern name. The station [opened in 1933 as _Enfield West_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakwood_tube_station) and was renamed _Oakwood_ in 1946, after the nearby Oakwood Park. Unlike most Underground names it hides no old etymology — it means transparently what it says, "oak wood."

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish |
| ------- | ------- |
| oak     | tammi   |
| wood    | metsä   |

Both words are plain: _oak_ is _tammi_, _wood_ is _metsä_. _Tammimetsä_ ("oak wood") is an ordinary Finnish descriptive, and the oak is well established in Finnish place-naming — the same _tammi_ that underlies [Acton](acton.md) → _Tammela_.

## Conclusion

Oakwood station in Finnish shall be called _Tammimetsä_.
