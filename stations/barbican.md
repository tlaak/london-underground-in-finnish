# Barbican

Barbican is served by the Circle, Hammersmith & City and Metropolitan lines, in the Cripplegate ward of the City of London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Barbican                   | Linnoitusportti     |

## Background

[The name comes from a _barbican_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbican_Estate) — from Low Latin _barbecana_, a fortified outpost or gateway, the outer defence of a city or castle, or a tower set over a gate. A Roman watchtower is thought to have stood on the street once called Barbican.

## Direct translations

| English  | Finnish         |
| -------- | --------------- |
| barbican | linnoitusportti |

A barbican is precisely a fortified gateway, and Finnish has a ready compound for it: _linnoitus_ (a fortification) plus _portti_ (a gate) — _linnoitusportti_. The translation is thus a direct rendering of the word's meaning.

## Conclusion

Barbican station in Finnish shall be called _Linnoitusportti_.
