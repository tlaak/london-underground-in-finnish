# Kings Cross St. Pancras

Kings Cross St. Pancras is served by the Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan, Northern, Piccadilly and Victoria lines, on the boundary of the London Boroughs of Camden and Islington.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Kings Cross St. Pancras    | Kuninkaanristi–Pyhä Pankratius |

## Background

The station's name joins two neighbouring places. [King's Cross takes its name from a monument to King George IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King%27s_Cross,_London) that stood from 1830 to 1845 at "the king's crossroads," a sixty-foot structure topped by a statue of the king. St Pancras, the adjoining parish, is named for its church, dedicated to [Saint Pancras of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Pancras) (Latin _Pancratius_), a boy martyred around AD 304.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish    |
| ------- | ---------- |
| King's  | kuninkaan  |
| cross   | risti      |
| Saint   | pyhä       |
| Pancras | Pankratius |

Both halves translate cleanly. "King's Cross" is a literal monument name — _kuninkaan risti_, compounded to _Kuninkaanristi_. "St Pancras" is the saint's name, which in Finnish is spelled [_Pankratius_](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pankratius), so "Saint Pancras" is _Pyhä Pankratius_. The two are joined as the English does, with a dash: _Kuninkaanristi–Pyhä Pankratius_.

## Conclusion

Kings Cross St. Pancras station in Finnish shall be called _Kuninkaanristi–Pyhä Pankratius_.
