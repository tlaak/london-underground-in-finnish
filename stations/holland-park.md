# Holland Park

Holland Park is served by the Central line, in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Holland Park               | Hollanninpuisto     |

## Background

The district is [named after Holland House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holland_Park), the Jacobean mansion (originally Cope Castle) whose grounds were sold off for housing; the area took the house's name. "Holland" here is the house's name — from the title of its owners, the Barons and Earls of Holland — rather than the country.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish  |
| ------- | -------- |
| Holland | Hollanti |
| park    | puisto   |

Although the "Holland" is a title rather than the Netherlands, there is no separate Finnish form for the name, so it stays as _Hollanti_. With _puisto_ ("park") the result is _Hollanninpuisto_, "Holland's park." This is one case where a plainer, near-transliterated name is the honest choice — there is no deeper etymology beneath the family title to translate.

## Conclusion

Holland Park station in Finnish shall be called _Hollanninpuisto_.
