# Perivale

Perivale is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Ealing in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Perivale                   | Päärynälaakso       |

## Background

[Perivale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perivale) is a compound of _perie_ (pear tree) and _vale_ (a wide valley) — "pear-tree valley." It is first recorded as _Pyryvale_ in 1508; before that the area was known as Little Greenford (_Greenford Parva_).

## Direct translations

| English              | Finnish |
| -------------------- | ------- |
| pear (tree), _perie_ | päärynä |
| vale, valley         | laakso  |

Both halves translate cleanly: _perie_ (pear) is _päärynä_, and _vale_ is _laakso_. The result, _Päärynälaakso_ ("pear valley"), reads as a perfectly natural Finnish place name.

## Conclusion

Perivale station in Finnish shall be called _Päärynälaakso_.
