# Roding Valley

Roding Valley is served by the Central line (Hainault loop), in the Epping Forest district of Essex.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Roding Valley              | Rootinginlaakso     |

## Background

The station takes its name from the valley of the River Roding. [The river and the Roding villages derive their name from _Hroða_, leader of the _Hroðingas_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodings) — "the people of Hroða," an Anglo-Saxon community that settled the area in the sixth century. The river was named after the villages, not the other way round.

## Direct translations

| English                 | Finnish  |
| ----------------------- | -------- |
| Roding (Hroða's people) | Rooting- |
| valley                  | laakso   |

"Roding" is a people-and-river name with no meaning to translate literally, so the sound is carried across as _Rooting-_, and _laakso_ ("valley") completes it: _Rootinginlaakso_, "the valley of the Rooding." The doubled vowel keeps the long English "o" and gives it a Finnish shape.

## Conclusion

Roding Valley station in Finnish shall be called _Rootinginlaakso_.
