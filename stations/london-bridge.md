# London Bridge

London Bridge is served by the Jubilee line and the Northern line (Bank branch), in the London Borough of Southwark, immediately south-east of the bridge itself.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| London Bridge              | Lontoonsilta        |

## Background

The station [takes its name from London Bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Bridge), the historic Thames crossing that has carried a bridge on roughly the same spot since Roman times. The name is plain description: the bridge at London.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish |
| ------- | ------- |
| London  | Lontoo  |
| bridge  | silta   |

London already has a settled Finnish exonym, [_Lontoo_](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lontoo), and _bridge_ is _silta_. Joined in the genitive, they give _Lontoonsilta_, "London's bridge" — the same shape as real Finnish bridge-names such as _Pitkäsilta_ in Helsinki.

## Conclusion

London Bridge station in Finnish shall be called _Lontoonsilta_.
