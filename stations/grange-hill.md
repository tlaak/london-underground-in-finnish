# Grange Hill

Grange Hill is served by the Central line (Hainault loop), on the boundary of the London Borough of Redbridge and Essex.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Grange Hill                | Tilanmäki           |

## Background

The name comes from a _grange_ — an outlying farm, originally one belonging to a manor or a monastery, with its barns and granary — that stood on the hill.

## Direct translations

| English                                         | Finnish       |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| [grange](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/grange) | maatila, tila |
| hill                                            | mäki          |

A _grange_ is a farm or farming estate, in Finnish _maatila_. The full literal form would be _Maatilanmäki_, "farm hill." But everyday Finnish clips long compound names, and _tila_ ("a holding, a farm") already carries the sense on its own, so the natural spoken form is the shorter _Tilanmäki_. It reads perfectly well as a Finnish place name.

## Conclusion

Grange Hill station in Finnish shall be called _Tilanmäki_, with the fuller _Maatilanmäki_ available where the extra clarity is wanted.
