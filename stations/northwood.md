# Northwood

Northwood is served by the Metropolitan line, in the London Borough of Hillingdon in North West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Northwood                  | Pohjolanmetsä       |

## Background

[Northwood is first recorded as _Northwode_ in 1435](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwood,_London), from Old English _north_ and _wode_ — "the northern wood," so called in relation to Ruislip and its woods. Unlike [Northolt](northolt.md), whose "-holt" is a false friend for a corner (_healh_), Northwood really is a wood.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish           |
| ------- | ----------------- |
| north   | pohjola, pohjois- |
| wood    | metsä             |

_Wood_ is _metsä_. For "north," rather than the plain prefix _pohjois-_, the evocative _Pohjola_ — the mythic Northland of the Kalevala — gives a fuller, name-like ring. The result is _Pohjolanmetsä_, "the northland's wood," and its genuine "wood" keeps it clearly distinct from Northolt's _Pohjoiskulma_ ("north corner").

## Conclusion

Northwood station in Finnish shall be called _Pohjolanmetsä_.
