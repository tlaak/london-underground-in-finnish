# Moor Park

Moor Park is served by the Metropolitan line, in the Three Rivers District of Hertfordshire, North West of London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Moor Park                  | Nevapuisto          |

## Background

Moor Park is named after the [Moor Park estate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moor_Park,_Hertfordshire), whose name derives from the old moorland here, part of the manor of the More. The ground belongs to the wet country around [Ruislip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruislip), whose own name means "the leaping place on the river where **rushes** grow" (Old English _rysc_ + _hlȳp_) — a rushy, open, waterlogged landscape rather than dry heath.

## Direct translations

| English      | Finnish          |
| ------------ | ---------------- |
| moor (marsh) | neva, suo, nummi |
| park         | puisto           |

_Park_ is _puisto_. As at [Morden](morden.md) and [Moorgate](moorgate.md), the "moor" here is wet ground, not the dry sandy heath that would take _nummi_. But rather than the generic _suo_ (any mire), the rushy, open character points to the more specific [_neva_](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/neva#Finnish) — an open, treeless mire, a former lake or pond overgrown by sedges, mosses and grasses. It is a real and common toponym element in Finland, seen in mire names across Ostrobothnia and in [Kauhaneva‑Pohjankangas National Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kauhaneva-Pohjankangas_National_Park).

## Conclusion

Because Moor Park's moor was open, rushy wetland, the station in Finnish shall be called _Nevapuisto_, "the open-mire park."
