# Gants Hill

Gants Hill is served by the Central line, in the Ilford district of the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Gants Hill                 | Gantinmäki          |

## Background

[Gants Hill likely takes its name from the _le Gant_ family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gants_Hill), stewards of Barking Abbey; the name appears as _Gantesgrave_ in 1291 and later settled into the modern "Gants Hill."

## Direct translations

| English            | Finnish |
| ------------------ | ------- |
| Gant (family name) | Gant    |
| hill               | mäki    |

The first element is a surname with no meaning to translate, so it is kept as-is. The generic _hill_ is _mäki_. In the genitive this gives _Gantinmäki_, "Gant's hill" — the same pattern as real Finnish farm- and hill-names.

## Conclusion

Gants Hill station in Finnish shall be called _Gantinmäki_.
