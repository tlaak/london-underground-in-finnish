# Wanstead

Wanstead is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Wanstead                   | Mäkelä              |

## Background

[Wanstead is recorded as _Wenstede_ in a charter of 1065](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wanstead), from Old English _wænn_, "a hill or mound," and _stede_, "a place or settlement" — "the homestead by the hill." (An alternative reads the first element as "wain/wagon," but the compound is then unclear.)

## Direct translations

| English            | Finnish |
| ------------------ | ------- |
| wænn (hill, mound) | mäki    |
| stede (place)      | -lä     |

Taking the favoured reading, "hill" plus "place/homestead" maps almost exactly onto Finnish _mäki_ + _-lä_. That yields [_Mäkelä_](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A4kel%C3%A4) — one of the most common of all real Finnish farm- and place-names, literally "the place by the hill." Few English station names land so neatly on a genuine, everyday Finnish toponym.

## Conclusion

Wanstead station in Finnish shall be called _Mäkelä_.
