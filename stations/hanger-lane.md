# Hanger Lane

Hanger Lane is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Ealing in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Hanger Lane                | Metsärinteenkuja    |

## Background

Hanger Lane takes its name from Hanger Hill, where ["hanger" is the Old English _hangra_, "a wooded slope"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanger_Lane) — a wood clinging to the side of a hill.

## Direct translations

| English           | Finnish    |
| ----------------- | ---------- |
| hanger (_hangra_) | metsärinne |
| lane              | kuja       |

_Hangra_, a wooded hillside, is neatly captured by _metsärinne_ ("forest slope"), and _lane_ is _kuja_, a narrow way. Together they give _Metsärinteenkuja_, "the lane of the wooded slope."

## Conclusion

Hanger Lane station in Finnish shall be called _Metsärinteenkuja_.
