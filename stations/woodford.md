# Woodford

Woodford is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Woodford                   | Metsävieska         |

## Background

[Woodford is Old English for "ford in or by the wood"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodford,_London) — the crossing where a minor Roman road forded the River Roding, until a bridge replaced it around 1238.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish           |
| ------- | ----------------- |
| wood    | metsä             |
| ford    | vieska (kahlaamo) |

_Wood_ is _metsä_. For _ford_, this project uses _vieska_, an old Finnish word for a shallow river-crossing, rather than the flat literal _kahlaamo_ — see [Greenford](greenford.md) for the full note on the convention. The result is _Metsävieska_, "wood ford."

## Conclusion

Woodford station in Finnish shall be called _Metsävieska_.
