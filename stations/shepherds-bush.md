# Shepherds Bush

Shepherds Bush is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Hammersmith and Fulham in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Shepherds Bush             | Paimenpensaikko     |

## Background

[The name most likely refers to the common land here being a resting point for shepherds driving their flocks to Smithfield Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shepherd%27s_Bush) in the City; it appears as "Sheppard's Bush Green" in 1635. The "bush" is the scrub of the common where the shepherds paused.

## Direct translations

| English  | Finnish           |
| -------- | ----------------- |
| shepherd | paimen            |
| bush     | pensas, pensaikko |

A shepherd is _paimen_ — not _lampuri_, which is a leasehold tenant farmer, a false friend the earlier translation reached for. The "bush" here is scrubland rather than a single shrub, so _pensaikko_ ("thicket, scrub") fits better than bare _pensas_. Together: _Paimenpensaikko_, "the shepherds' scrub." (This corrects the earlier _Lampurinpensaikko_.)

## Conclusion

Shepherds Bush station in Finnish shall be called _Paimenpensaikko_.
