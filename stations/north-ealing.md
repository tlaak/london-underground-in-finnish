# North Ealing

North Ealing is served by the Piccadilly line, in the London Borough of Ealing in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| North Ealing               | Pohjois-Killanki    |

## Background

North Ealing is the northern part of [Ealing](ealing-broadway.md), whose name comes from the _Gillingas_, "the people of Gilla."

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish  |
| ------- | -------- |
| north   | pohjois- |
| Ealing  | Killanki |

This is simply [Ealing](ealing-broadway.md) → _Killanki_ with the directional prefix _pohjois-_ ("north"). Following the project's convention for directional compounds — as in [Itä-Tammela](acton.md) and [Pohjois-Tammela](acton.md) — it is written with a hyphen: _Pohjois-Killanki_.

## Conclusion

North Ealing station in Finnish shall be called _Pohjois-Killanki_.
