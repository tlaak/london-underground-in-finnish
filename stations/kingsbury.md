# Kingsbury

Kingsbury is served by the Jubilee line, in the London Borough of Brent in North West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Kingsbury                  | Kuninkaanlinna      |

## Background

[Kingsbury means "the king's fortification"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingsbury,_London) — Old English _cyninges_ (king's) and _burh_, a fortified place or stronghold.

## Direct translations

| English             | Finnish   |
| ------------------- | --------- |
| king's (_cyninges_) | kuninkaan |
| _burh_ (stronghold) | linna     |

The key is _burh_. It is not a "town" (_kaupunki_) but a fortified place, for which the Finnish is _linna_ — the very element in real castle-towns like Hämeenlinna and Savonlinna. So Kingsbury becomes _Kuninkaanlinna_, "the king's castle," rather than the looser "king's town."

## Conclusion

Kingsbury station in Finnish shall be called _Kuninkaanlinna_.
