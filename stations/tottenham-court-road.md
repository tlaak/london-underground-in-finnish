# Tottenham Court Road

Tottenham Court Road is served by the Central and Northern lines, on the boundary of the London Boroughs of Camden and Westminster.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Tottenham Court Road       | Tottilankartanontie |

## Background

The road is named after the Manor of Tottenham Court. [The earliest record is _Þottanheale_ around AD 1000 — "Totta's nook" — evolving through _Totehele_ (1086) and _Totenhale_ to _Totenhale Court_ by 1487](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tottenham_Court_Road), later popularly "Tottenham Court." Despite the shared look, it is **not** related to the district of Tottenham in Haringey.

## Direct translations

| English        | Finnish |
| -------------- | ------- |
| Totta (person) | Tottila |
| Court (manor)  | kartano |
| road           | tie     |

"Totta's" place becomes _Tottila_ (the _Totta-_ sound plus the _-la_ settlement suffix). The "Court" here means a manor house, which is _kartano_, and "road" is _tie_. Chained in the genitive: _Tottilankartanontie_, "the road of Totta's manor."

## Conclusion

Tottenham Court Road station in Finnish shall be called _Tottilankartanontie_.
