# Loughton

Loughton is served by the Central line, in the Epping Forest district of Essex.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Loughton                   | Lohtila             |

## Background

[The name Loughton is Old English and means the farm or settlement of someone called Luhha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loughton) — Luhha's _tūn_, a personal name plus the common habitation suffix.

## Direct translations

| English            | Finnish |
| ------------------ | ------- |
| Luhha (person)     | Lohti-  |
| -ton (_tūn_, farm) | -la     |

This is a person's farmstead, so the honest approach is a name that echoes the sound and marks a settlement. _Lohti-_ carries the "Luhha/Lough-" sound, and the Finnish _-la_ suffix is the exact counterpart of Old English _-tūn_, "farmstead of." The result, _Lohtila_, reads as a natural Finnish farm-name of the same shape as the English.

## Conclusion

Loughton station in Finnish shall be called _Lohtila_.
