# Lancaster Gate

Lancaster Gate is served by the Central line, in the City of Westminster, by Kensington Gardens.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Lancaster Gate             | Jokilinnanportti    |

## Background

The area is named after [Lancaster Gate, a nearby entrance to Kensington Gardens, named in honour of Queen Victoria as Duke of Lancaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_Gate). The place-name Lancaster itself is older: [recorded as _Loncastre_ in 1086](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster,_Lancashire), from "Lon" (the River Lune) and Old English _cæster_ / Latin _castrum_, "the Roman fort on the Lune."

## Direct translations

| English                 | Finnish   |
| ----------------------- | --------- |
| Lancaster (Lune + fort) | jokilinna |
| gate                    | portti    |

Rather than transliterate the ducal title, this translation reaches through to Lancaster's own meaning, "the fort on the river." _Jokilinna_ ("river castle/fort," _joki_ + _linna_) captures both the Lune and the _castrum_, and _portti_ is "gate." The result is _Jokilinnanportti_, "the gate of the river-fort."

## Conclusion

Lancaster Gate station in Finnish shall be called _Jokilinnanportti_.
