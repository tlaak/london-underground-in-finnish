# Redbridge

Redbridge is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Redbridge                  | Punasilta           |

## Background

The name is quite literal: [it comes from a bridge over the River Roding, demolished in 1921, made of red brick, unlike other bridges in the area which were of white stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redbridge,_London). The red bridge (earlier Hocklee's Bridge) gave its name to the crossing, and later to the borough.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish         |
| ------- | --------------- |
| red     | puna-, punainen |
| bridge  | silta           |

A plain descriptive name, so a plain compound serves. Finnish toponyms take the combining form _puna-_ rather than the full adjective _punainen_ — exactly as in the Helsinki district [_Punavuori_](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punavuori), "red hill." By the same pattern "red bridge" is _Punasilta_. (This corrects the earlier two-word _Punainensilta_, which is not a natural Finnish compound.)

## Conclusion

Redbridge station in Finnish shall be called _Punasilta_.
