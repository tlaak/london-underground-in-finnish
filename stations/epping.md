# Epping

Epping is the northern terminus of the Central line, in the Epping Forest district of Essex.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Epping                     | Epilä               |

## Background

[The name Epping derives from the Old English _yppeingas_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epping,_Essex), "the people of the raised place" — a settlement of people living by an upland or lookout, on the higher ground above the surrounding forest.

## Direct translations

| English                    | Finnish |
| -------------------------- | ------- |
| Epping (people of, -ingas) | -lä     |

There is no clean word-for-word rendering: the name is a people-name, "those of the raised place." Following the project's preference for a real Finnish toponym that echoes the sound over a stiff literal, [_Epilä_](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epil%C3%A4) — a district of Tampere — is an close phonetic match, and its _-lä_ suffix is exactly the Finnish marker of a place of habitation, mirroring the _-ingas_ "people of" sense.

## Conclusion

Epping station in Finnish shall be called _Epilä_.
