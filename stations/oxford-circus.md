# Oxford Circus

Oxford Circus is served by the Bakerloo, Central and Victoria lines, at the junction of Oxford Street and Regent Street in the West End, in the City of Westminster.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Oxford Circus              | Härkävieskanaukio   |

## Background

Oxford Circus is [the round road junction where Oxford Street meets Regent Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Circus); a _circus_, in this sense, is a circular junction, laid out by John Nash. It takes its name from Oxford Street, the great thoroughfare that ran west out of London towards the city of [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford) — itself Old English _Oxenaforda_, "ford of the oxen," a shallow river-crossing for cattle.

## Direct translations

| English      | Finnish           |
| ------------ | ----------------- |
| ox (_oxena_) | härkä             |
| ford         | vieska (kahlaamo) |
| circus       | aukio             |

The name to translate is the underlying Oxford: _ox_ is _härkä_ and _ford_ is _vieska_ (the project's element for fords, as in [Greenford](greenford.md) → _Vihervieska_), giving _Härkävieska_ for "ox-ford." A _circus_ — a circular open junction — is an _aukio_ ("square, open place"). Joined, they make _Härkävieskanaukio_.

## Conclusion

Oxford Circus station in Finnish shall be called _Härkävieskanaukio_.
