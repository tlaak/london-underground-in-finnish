# Mile End

Mile End is served by the Central, District and Hammersmith & City lines, in the London Borough of Tower Hamlets in the East End.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Mile End                   | Mailinpää           |

## Background

[Mile End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mile_End) is formed from Middle English _mile_ and _ende_ and means "the hamlet a mile away" — a mile out from Aldgate and the City, along the old London-to-Colchester road.

## Direct translations

| English | Finnish |
| ------- | ------- |
| mile    | maili   |
| end     | pää     |

_Mile_ is the loanword _maili_, and _end_ — in the sense of the far end of something — is _pää_ (which is also literally "head"). _Mailinpää_, "mile's end," keeps the plain measured-distance sense of the original.

## Conclusion

Mile End station in Finnish shall be called _Mailinpää_.
