# Notting Hill Gate

Notting Hill Gate is served by the Central, Circle and District lines, in the Royal Borough of Kensington and Chelsea in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Notting Hill Gate          | Knottilanmäenportti |

## Background

The name is uncertain, but [the most likely origin is the Saxon personal name _Cnotta_ with the _-ing_ suffix "a group or settlement of people,"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Notting_Hill) recorded as _Knottynghull_ in 1356 — "the hill of Cnotta's people." The "Gate" was a tollgate on the road west.

## Direct translations

| English                   | Finnish  |
| ------------------------- | -------- |
| Notting (Cnotta's people) | Knottila |
| hill                      | mäki     |
| gate                      | portti   |

"Cnotta's people" becomes _Knottila_ — the _Cnotta/Knott-_ sound plus the _-la_ suffix marking a place of people, mirroring the _-ing_. With _mäki_ ("hill") and _portti_ ("gate") in genitive chain, the whole is _Knottilanmäenportti_, "the gate of Knottila hill."

## Conclusion

Notting Hill Gate station in Finnish shall be called _Knottilanmäenportti_.
