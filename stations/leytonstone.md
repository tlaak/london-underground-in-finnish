# Leytonstone

Leytonstone is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Waltham Forest in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Leytonstone                | Jokelanpylväs       |

## Background

[Leytonstone was originally "Leyton-atte-Stone"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leytonstone) — Leyton by the stone. The stone in question is the High Stone, a restored 18th-century obelisk on an earlier stump, traditionally held to be a Roman milestone. So the name is [Leyton](leyton.md) distinguished by a standing marker.

## Direct translations

| English             | Finnish |
| ------------------- | ------- |
| Leyton              | Jokela  |
| stone (the obelisk) | pylväs  |

Building on [Leyton](leyton.md) → _Jokela_, the second element is the marker itself. Because the High Stone is really an obelisk — a standing pillar rather than a loose rock — _pylväs_ ("pillar, column, obelisk") fits it far better than a plain _kivi_ ("stone"). The compound is _Jokelanpylväs_, "Jokela's pillar."

## Conclusion

Leytonstone station in Finnish shall be called _Jokelanpylväs_.
