# Monument

Monument is served by the Circle and District lines, in the City of London near the northern end of London Bridge.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Monument                   | Monumentti          |

## Background

The station is [named after the Monument to the Great Fire of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monument_to_the_Great_Fire_of_London), the tall Doric column raised by Christopher Wren and Robert Hooke to commemorate the fire of 1666. The station opened in 1884 and takes the landmark's name directly.

## Direct translations

| English  | Finnish    |
| -------- | ---------- |
| monument | monumentti |

There is nothing to interpret: _monument_ is _monumentti_, a direct loanword in Finnish. Because the station is named after one specific named structure — _the_ Monument — the Finnish simply carries that word across as a proper name, exactly as with [Bank](bank.md) → _Suomen Pankki_.

## Conclusion

Monument station in Finnish shall be called _Monumentti_.
