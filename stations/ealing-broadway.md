# Ealing Broadway

Ealing Broadway is served by the Central and District lines, in the London Borough of Ealing in West London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Ealing Broadway            | Killangin maantie   |

## Background

[Ealing takes its name from the _Gillingas_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ealing), a Saxon group named after a chief called _Gilla_ — "the people of Gilla" — recorded around 700 and appearing as _Yllinges_ by about 1170. "Broadway" is the broad main road at the centre of the town; the station was renamed from plain "Ealing" to Ealing Broadway in 1875.

## Direct translations

| English                 | Finnish  |
| ----------------------- | -------- |
| Ealing (Gilla's people) | Killanki |
| broadway (broad road)   | maantie  |

Ealing is built on the personal name _Gilla_, which has no Finnish meaning to translate, so — as with [Angel](angel.md) → _Angeli_ — it is Finnicised by sound: _Killanki_ keeps the shape of _Ealing_/_Gilling_ with a natural Finnish ending. "Broadway," a broad thoroughfare, is _maantie_ (a highway or main road). In the genitive this gives _Killangin maantie_, "Ealing's main road."

## Conclusion

Ealing Broadway station in Finnish shall be called _Killangin maantie_.
