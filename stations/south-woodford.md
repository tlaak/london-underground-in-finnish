# South Woodford

South Woodford is served by the Central line, in the London Borough of Redbridge in East London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| South Woodford             | Etelä-Metsävieska   |

## Background

[South Woodford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Woodford) is the southern part of Woodford, historically the parish of Woodford St Mary. It shares [Woodford](woodford.md)'s etymology: "ford in or by the wood."

## Direct translations

| English  | Finnish     |
| -------- | ----------- |
| south    | etelä-      |
| Woodford | Metsävieska |

This is simply [Woodford](woodford.md) → _Metsävieska_ with the directional prefix _etelä-_ ("south"). Following the project's convention for directional compounds — as in the Actons, [Itä-Tammela](acton.md) and [Länsi-Tammela](acton.md) — it is written with a hyphen: _Etelä-Metsävieska_.

## Conclusion

South Woodford station in Finnish shall be called _Etelä-Metsävieska_.
