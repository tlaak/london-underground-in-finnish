# Moorgate

Moorgate is served by the Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan and Northern lines, in the City of London.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Moorgate                   | Suoportti           |

## Background

Moorgate was one of the northern gates in the City of London wall, and [it took its name from Moorfields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moorgate) — the ground immediately outside. Crucially, Moorfields was not dry heath but "an area of marshy land," the "once very marshy" Lower and Little Moorfields, so waterlogged that it was not built over until 1817.

## Direct translations

| English      | Finnish    |
| ------------ | ---------- |
| moor (marsh) | suo, nummi |
| gate         | portti     |

_Gate_ is _portti_. The "moor" is the crux. Elsewhere this project renders "moor" as _nummi_, a dry sandy heath — but, exactly as at [Morden](morden.md), that is the wrong ground here: Moorfields was a genuine marsh, so the accurate word is _suo_ (mire, marsh). This corrects the earlier _Nummiportti_ to _Suoportti_.

## Conclusion

Because Moorfields was truly marshy, Moorgate station in Finnish shall be called _Suoportti_, "marsh gate."
