# Buckhurst Hill

Buckhurst Hill is served by the Central line (Epping branch), in the Epping Forest district of Essex, just outside the Greater London boundary.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Buckhurst Hill             | Pyökkimäki          |

## Background

[Buckhurst Hill is Old English and means "a hill covered with beech trees"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckhurst_Hill) — from _bōc_ (beech) and _hyrst_ (a wooded hill). It is first recorded in the 12th century as _La Bocherste_, passing through _Bucket Hill_ before reaching the modern spelling.

## Direct translations

| English               | Finnish |
| --------------------- | ------- |
| beech (_bōc_)         | pyökki  |
| wooded hill (_hyrst_) | mäki    |

Beech is _pyökki_ in Finnish (the tree grows only at the very edge of its range in southern Finland, but the word is well established). _Hyrst_, a wooded hill, is naturally _mäki_. The two give _Pyökkimäki_, "beech hill" — a straightforward and natural Finnish name.

## Conclusion

Buckhurst Hill station in Finnish shall be called _Pyökkimäki_.
