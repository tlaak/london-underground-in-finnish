# Old Street

Old Street is served by the Northern line (City branch), on the boundary of the London Boroughs of Islington and Hackney.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Old Street                 | Vanhatie            |

## Background

[Old Street is recorded as _Ealdestrate_ around 1200](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Street) and _le Oldestrete_ in 1373 — literally "the old street." It follows the line of an old Roman or possibly pre-Roman track between Silchester and Colchester, and the name marks its evident antiquity.

## Direct translations

| English               | Finnish   |
| --------------------- | --------- |
| old (_eald_)          | vanha     |
| street/road (_stræt_) | tie, katu |

_Eald_ is _vanha_ (old). For _stræt_, the reference is an ancient road or track rather than a paved town street, so _tie_ (road) fits better than _katu_ (street). The result is _Vanhatie_, "old road."

## Conclusion

Old Street station in Finnish shall be called _Vanhatie_.
