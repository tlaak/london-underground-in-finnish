# Debden

Debden is served by the Central line (Epping branch), in the civil parish of Loughton in the Epping Forest district of Essex.

## Summary

| London Underground station | Finnish translation |
| -------------------------- | ------------------- |
| Debden                     | Syvälaakso          |

## Background

[Debden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debden,_Epping_Forest) is recorded as _Deppedana_ / _Depeduna_ in the Domesday Book of 1086, and was commonly written _Depden_ until the Napoleonic era. It is formed from Old English _dēop_ ("deep") and _denu_ ("valley") — "deep valley."

## Direct translations

| English         | Finnish |
| --------------- | ------- |
| deep (_dēop_)   | syvä    |
| valley (_denu_) | laakso  |

Both elements are plain: _dēop_ is _syvä_ and _denu_ is _laakso_. Together they give _Syvälaakso_, "deep valley" — an ordinary and natural Finnish place name.

## Conclusion

Debden station in Finnish shall be called _Syvälaakso_.
