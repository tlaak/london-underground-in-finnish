# List of all lines

Finnish translations of the London Underground line names.

| London Underground line | Finnish translation      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Bakerloo                | Leipuriaho               |
| Central                 | Keskusta                 |
| Circle                  | Kehä                     |
| District                | Piirikunta               |
| Hammersmith & City      | Vasarapaja–Kantakaupunki |
| Jubilee                 | Hopeajuhla               |
| Metropolitan            | Metropoli                |
| Northern                | Pohjola                  |
| Piccadilly              | Pikkurilli               |
| Victoria                | Viktoria                 |
| Waterloo & City         | Vesiaho–Kantakaupunki    |

## Notes

- **Bakerloo → _Leipuriaho_** — the name is [a portmanteau of **Baker** Street and Water**loo**](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bakerloo_line). The Finnish reproduces the same splice: _Leipuri_ ("baker") + the _-aho_ from [Waterloo → _Vesiaho_](stations/waterloo.md).
- **Central → _Keskusta_** — [named for running through the centre of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_line); _keskusta_ is "the centre, downtown."
- **Circle → _Kehä_** — a circular route. _Kehä_ is "ring," the word in Helsinki's own circular railway, the [Kehärata](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kehärata) ("Ring Rail Line").
- **District → _Piirikunta_** — from the old [Metropolitan **District** Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_line); _piirikunta_ is an administrative "district." The fuller form is deliberate: bare _piiri_ can also mean a circle/ring, a sense that belongs to the Circle line's _Kehä_.
- **Hammersmith & City → _Vasarapaja–Kantakaupunki_** — Hammersmith most likely means ["(place with) a **hammer smithy**," a forge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hammersmith) (Old English _hamor_ + _smiððe_), giving _vasarapaja_ ("hammer-smithy"); "City" takes the same _Kantakaupunki_ as [Waterloo & City](stations/waterloo.md).
- **Jubilee → _Hopeajuhla_** — [opened in 1977 to mark the **Silver** Jubilee of Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jubilee_line); _hopeajuhla_ is "silver celebration."
- **Metropolitan → _Metropoli_** — the first Underground line, the [Metropolitan Railway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metropolitan_line); rendered by the plain Finnish _metropoli_ ("metropolis"), deliberately not _Metropolia_ (a Helsinki university of applied sciences).
- **Northern → _Pohjola_** — [the Northern line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_line). Rather than the plain adjective _pohjoinen_, the evocative [_Pohjola_](<https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohjola_(mytologia)>) — the mythic Northland of the Kalevala — gives a fuller, name-like ring (and echoes the real transport name [Pohjolan Liikenne](https://fi.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pohjolan_Liikenne)). It matches [Northwood → _Pohjolanmetsä_](stations/northwood.md).
- **Piccadilly → _Pikkurilli_** — named from the [**piccadill**, a fashionable stiff ruff/collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccadilly) that made the tailor Robert Baker rich and gave his "Piccadilly Hall" its mocking name. That garment sense doesn't translate into anything name-like, so this falls back to phonetics: _Pikkurilli_ ("little finger," _pikku_ + _rilli_) is a close sound-match to "Piccadilly." The original name is itself a bit absurd, and the Finnish keeps that spirit while landing on a real everyday word.
- **Victoria → _Viktoria_** — named for [Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_line); the royal name is kept in its Finnish spelling, _Viktoria_.
- **Waterloo & City → _Vesiaho–Kantakaupunki_** — see [Waterloo](stations/waterloo.md).
